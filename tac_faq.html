Feeling Lost? Frequently Asked Questions:

What is TAC?

Trojan Anime Culture! Other names: Trojan Anime Club, Trojan Alcohol Culture, Trojan Axing Company, Trojan Automobile Culture, Trojan Anl Cndoms, Trojan *hegao Culture, and Trojans Actually Crying.

What does TAC do?

We are, believe it or not, an anime club. We watch anime, garbage, ゴミ, and the occasional good show.

What goes down on Thursday meetings?

We pick a theme (usually 2 days before), members pitch shows on Discord, and we vote on 2-3 episodes to watch depending on time. Afterwards, we finish games like Jeopardy or anime music trivia!

When and where does TAC meet?

General Meetings are on Thursdays from 8-10 PM, and we also have bi-weekly field trips on weekends. Location is usually in THH but can vary—check announcements on Discord for details!

How do I pitch anime?

After announcements in the thursday requests chat, drop your pitch there! Make sure it’s not in the Watched Anime list (no repeats within two years).

Does TAC have events?

Absolutely! We have bi-weekly field trips to places such as Little Tokyo, Round 1 Japanese Arcade, conventions, and more depending on the semester. We also have our traditional end-of-semester dinner. Check announcements for updates!

How do I become an officer?

Be a regular and friendly face! We have elections halfway through spring semester.

How do I get anime?
